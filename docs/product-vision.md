# WorkTwin Product Vision

## What WorkTwin is

WorkTwin is a privacy-first AI work, onboarding, policy, workflow and learning companion for employees in regulated SMEs.

It helps employees understand approved company documents, follow internal processes, practise role-specific scenarios, prepare for check-ins and ask work questions safely.

The employer gets useful organisational learning signals, but not private employee conversations.

## The regulated-SME opportunity

Many regulated SMEs already have the information their teams need in handbooks, policies, SOPs, process notes and training documents. The problem is that employees often cannot find the right answer, do not know which document applies, or feel uncomfortable asking basic questions.

That creates repeated manager interruptions, slower onboarding, inconsistent process knowledge, avoidable mistakes and weak evidence that staff understand important workflows.

WorkTwin focuses on teams where policy, compliance, workflow consistency and trust matter, but where managers do not have the time or budget for heavy enterprise learning and knowledge systems.

## Privacy-first and employee-first model

WorkTwin is designed as employee support, not employee surveillance.

The product rules are:

- no employee surveillance
- no performance scoring
- no productivity tracking
- no sentiment scoring
- no manager access to private employee chat transcripts by default
- no real staff, service-user, client or private data in demo files

Employees should be able to ask questions without fear that a private learning moment will be turned into a management record.

## Why employer insights must be anonymised

Employers still need to understand where policies, onboarding and training are failing. WorkTwin can support that by showing anonymised and aggregated trends, such as repeated policy confusion, unanswered document gaps, common onboarding questions and topics where extra training may help.

Those insights must never expose private employee chat transcripts, private notes, individual names, individual questions, performance judgements or small-group data that could identify a person.

This is central to the trust model. The employer can improve the system without watching the individual.

## Future vertical strategy

WorkTwin starts with one focused vertical: UK care providers.

Future verticals may include:

- financial advice firms
- property management companies
- recruitment agencies
- healthcare admin providers
- training providers
- regulated service SMEs

Each vertical should keep the same core privacy, source-citation and escalation rules while adapting workflows, document templates, terminology, roles and scenario examples to the sector.

## Core platform vs vertical templates

The core WorkTwin platform provides the shared foundation:

- private employee AI assistant
- approved-document question answering
- source-cited responses
- onboarding and learning journeys
- scenario practice
- private notes
- role-based document access
- sensitive-topic escalation
- anonymised employer insights

Vertical templates sit on top of that platform. They provide sector-specific content structures, examples, role labels, workflows, escalation categories and demo journeys.

WorkTwin Care Pilot is one vertical template. It is not the full product boundary.
