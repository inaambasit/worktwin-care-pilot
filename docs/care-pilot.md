# WorkTwin Care Pilot

## What WorkTwin Care Pilot is

WorkTwin Care Pilot is the first vertical demo of the broader WorkTwin platform.

It adapts WorkTwin for UK care providers by using care-specific policies, onboarding tasks, escalation topics and practice scenarios. The aim is to test whether a private AI work, policy and learning companion can help care staff find approved guidance, build confidence and reduce repeated manager interruptions.

Care is the first pilot vertical. It is not the full product boundary.

## Why care providers are first

Care providers are a strong first vertical because they combine:

- high compliance and documentation requirements
- frequent onboarding needs
- repeated operational questions
- sensitive topics that must route to a human
- real consequences when policies are misunderstood
- stretched managers who need staff to find trusted guidance quickly

The care setting makes the privacy promise especially important. Staff must be able to ask basic, sensitive or confidence-building questions without feeling watched.

## Why Thumhara Centre is the first internal test environment

Thumhara Centre is being used as the first internal test environment for a focused, realistic care-provider demo.

The demo uses sample content only. It must not include real staff, service-user, client or private care data.

The internal test should validate:

- whether the product story is clear
- whether employee and admin views feel safely separated
- whether privacy wording builds trust
- whether care-specific workflows are recognisable
- whether anonymised insights are useful without exposing individuals
- whether sensitive topics consistently guide employees to human escalation

## Care-specific workflows in the demo

The current care demo uses these workflows:

- medication refusal
- safeguarding
- incident reporting
- sickness policy
- complaints
- onboarding
- scenario practice

These examples are care-specific templates on top of the shared WorkTwin platform.

## Safety and privacy rules

WorkTwin Care Pilot must follow the platform rules:

- no employee surveillance
- no performance scoring
- no productivity tracking
- no sentiment scoring
- no manager access to private employee chat transcripts by default
- employer dashboards show anonymised and aggregated trends only
- answers are source-cited from approved documents
- safeguarding, medication, health and safety, complaints, HR, disciplinary, legal, payroll and wellbeing concerns trigger human escalation guidance

The care vertical should demonstrate these rules clearly before backend or RAG integration begins.
