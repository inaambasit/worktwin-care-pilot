# Thumhara Trusted-User Pilot Pack

**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Status:** Controlled internal pilot — not production, not live operational deployment.

---

## 1. Pilot status

WorkTwin Care Pilot is in controlled pilot preparation. It is not live operational software. It is not approved for real service-user decision-making of any kind.

The current proof is based on a four-policy staff-visible set:

- Visitor Sign-In and Identification
- Mobile Phone and Portable Device Use
- Confidentiality and Information Handling
- Infection Prevention and Basic Hygiene

Authentication and session proof has passed locally using a non-real dedicated staff test account. No real staff have been granted access yet. No real service-user data, HR data, safeguarding records, medication records, complaints data or confidential personal information should be entered into the system at any point.

---

## 2. Who may use the pilot

- 3–5 named, trusted users only, nominated by Thumhara Centre leadership.
- Every user must be individually briefed before access is granted.
- Every user must understand this is a controlled test environment, not a production system.
- Users must not invite others, share their login or forward access details to anyone.
- Access should be removed immediately if there is any uncertainty about safe use, or if any misuse occurs.

---

## 3. What users can ask

Safe questions are general, policy- or workflow-based, and contain no real names, case details or live incident information. Examples of safe questions:

- How should a visitor sign in?
- What details should be recorded in the visitor log?
- Can staff use their personal mobile phone during work?
- What practical steps should staff follow when handling Thumhara Centre records?
- When should staff wash their hands during normal day-to-day work?
- What should staff do to keep visitor records secure?
- Where can I find the escalation contacts?

Questions should relate to general policy, process or workflow. They should not include names, case details or any live situation.

---

## 4. What users must not enter

The following must never be typed into Ask WorkTwin or any part of the system:

- Service-user names
- Family member names
- Staff names
- Medical details or diagnoses
- Safeguarding allegations or case details
- Medication incidents, doses or administration details
- Complaint details involving named individuals
- HR issues, disciplinary or grievance information
- Addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, NHS numbers, National Insurance numbers or any other personal identifiers
- Confidential documents or extracts pasted into Ask WorkTwin
- Urgent or live incident details

**Unsafe examples — do not type these:**

- "John Smith missed his medication, what should I do?"
- "A service user says they are being abused by [name], what should I do?"
- "I want to complain about [named staff member]."
- "Can I tell [family member name] about [service-user name]?"
- "A resident has fallen and is confused, what should I do?"

If you are unsure whether a question is safe, do not type it. Contact the pilot lead instead.

---

## 5. Escalation boundaries

WorkTwin must not replace human judgement, managers, safeguarding leads, medication leads, clinical professionals, HR, legal advisers or emergency services.

The following issues must always be escalated to the appropriate human lead and must not be handled through WorkTwin:

- Safeguarding concerns or allegations
- Medication queries, incidents or errors
- Falls, accidents or incidents involving service users or staff
- Immediate danger or emergency situations
- Medical symptoms or health emergencies
- Uncertainty about sharing confidential information with family members or third parties
- HR matters, named staff concerns or employment issues
- Complaints involving named individuals
- Legal or compliance uncertainty
- Wellbeing or mental health concerns

**If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.**

---

## 6. What WorkTwin is expected to do in this pilot

- Answer safe, general day-to-day questions from the approved staff-visible policy set.
- Return policy-grounded, source-grounded answers where a relevant approved policy is available.
- Escalate or decline high-risk questions before generating an answer.
- Help users identify the right policy direction or the right person to contact.
- Support onboarding and consistency by making approved policy content easier to find.

---

## 7. What WorkTwin is not expected to do

- It is not a replacement for policies, managers or professional judgement.
- It is not a clinical, legal, HR, safeguarding or medication adviser.
- It does not approve actions or decisions.
- It does not make decisions about care or about individual service users.
- It must not be used for emergencies.
- It must not be treated as production software or relied upon for live operational decisions.

## 9. Incident process during pilot

If someone accidentally enters real personal data, service-user data, HR information, safeguarding details, medication information, complaint details or other confidential information:

1. Stop testing immediately for that user or session.
2. Tell the pilot lead straight away.
3. Do not copy, forward or repeat the data further.
4. Record only a minimal incident note — do not repeat the sensitive details in the note itself.
5. Review whether the user's access should be paused pending review.
6. Delete or purge the data from the system if applicable, once the storage and logging position has been confirmed.

Any accidental entry of personal data must be treated as a pilot incident and documented.

## 10. Access checklist before inviting a user

Before any trusted user is granted access, confirm all of the following:

- [ ] User is named and individually trusted by Thumhara Centre leadership.
- [ ] User has been briefed and has read this pilot pack.
- [ ] User understands that no real personal or service-user data should be entered.
- [ ] User understands the escalation rules and boundaries.
- [ ] User knows the feedback route.
- [ ] User has the correct account and access credentials.
- [ ] User knows how to log out.
- [ ] Pilot lead knows the user has been granted access.
- [ ] Start and end date for the user's access has been agreed.
- [ ] Access removal plan has been agreed.

Do not grant access until every item on this checklist is confirmed.
