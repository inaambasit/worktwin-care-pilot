## 3. Purpose of This Extract

This extract is a shorter, WorkTwin-facing version of the Confidentiality and Information Handling Policy. It is prepared to support future controlled AI testing under the WorkTwin care pilot, once governance approval has been obtained.

This extract is designed to:

- Help staff understand what information they may and may not share, and with whom
- Remind staff what must never be entered into WorkTwin or any other non-approved system
- Describe safe information-handling behaviours for day-to-day care work
- Guide staff who suspect a confidentiality breach or accidental data disclosure
- Block and redirect questions about real personal data, disclosures, or identity-linked information to the correct human route
- Reinforce that real care records and personal information must be kept in Thumhara Centre's approved care-recording system, not in WorkTwin

This extract does not replace the source policy, data protection law, the guidance of Thumhara Centre's data-protection responsible person, or any specialist regulatory requirement.

## 4. WorkTwin Lane

**Lane B — cautious staff-visible support, with escalation and block rules.**

This extract is assigned to Lane B. Confidentiality questions can involve decisions about disclosure, data-subject rights, potential regulatory consequences, or breach response — all of which require human judgement and cannot safely be delegated to a general guidance tool. WorkTwin may support staff understanding of general information-handling principles but must escalate and block where a human decision is needed.

Lane B applies because the risk of incorrect guidance here is not only to service users but also to the organisation and to staff themselves. A general answer given in error about whether to share information could cause a reportable data breach.

---

## 5. What WorkTwin May Answer

WorkTwin may draw on this extract to give **general information-handling guidance** once governance approval is in place and the document has been uploaded and indexed. WorkTwin may answer:

- **General confidentiality principles** — what confidentiality means in a care setting and why it matters
- **What counts as confidential information** — a general description of the categories of information that are confidential in care work (personal, health, financial, family, care-plan related)
- **What staff should not share or discuss** — reminders about not discussing service users in public, in informal settings, or with people who do not have a legitimate need to know
- **What must never be entered into WorkTwin** — clear reminders of the no-real-data boundary
- **General personal device rules** — that personal devices, screenshots, copied notes and informal messaging are not approved methods of recording or sharing service-user information
- **Who to contact if a staff member is uncertain** — a prompt to contact their senior or registered manager before acting on a disclosure or sharing decision
- A reminder to **record information only in approved systems** — Thumhara Centre's care management system, not WorkTwin

Answers must be calm, brief, and principle-focused. WorkTwin must not attempt to advise on specific disclosures, specific service users, or specific regulatory decisions.

## 6. What WorkTwin Must Escalate

WorkTwin must stop attempting to advise and direct the worker to a human immediately when the question involves any of the following. These are **escalation-guided** topics — WorkTwin points to the route; it does not resolve the situation:

| Situation | Required WorkTwin behaviour | Route |
|-----------|----------------------------|-------|
| Staff member suspects a confidentiality breach has occurred | Stop. Direct staff not to act alone and to contact their senior immediately. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Staff member has accidentally shared information with the wrong person | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior immediately and not to attempt to retrieve or correct the disclosure without guidance. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| A service user, family member or third party has asked for disclosure of information | Stop. Direct staff not to make a disclosure decision alone. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Staff member is unsure whether they are permitted to share a piece of information | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior before sharing anything. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Staff member thinks another staff member has handled information incorrectly | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior. Do not advise the staff member to challenge colleagues directly. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| A subject access request or data rights request has been made | Stop. Direct staff to inform the registered manager. WorkTwin must not attempt to guide a response to a legal data request. | Registered Manager → data-protection responsible person (if applicable) |

*All contact details are placeholders and must be confirmed by Thumhara Centre before this extract is approved for use.*

## 7. What WorkTwin Must Block and Route

For the following topics WorkTwin must **block direct guidance and route to the correct human or specialist** without attempting to advise on the substance:

| Blocked topic | WorkTwin behaviour | Route |
|---------------|--------------------|-------|
| Requests involving real service-user names, identifiers or personal details | Block. Do not process. Remind staff not to enter real personal data. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Questions asking WorkTwin to decide whether information may be shared with a named person | Block. WorkTwin cannot make disclosure decisions. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Requests for medication information, care-plan detail or clinical information about a real person | Block. Do not advise. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Safeguarding details, allegations or case information | Block. Do not advise. | Safeguarding Lead → Registered Manager |
| Incident or complaint details | Block. Do not advise. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| HR, disciplinary, payroll, absence or performance details relating to any individual | Block. Do not advise. | HR / people route → Line Manager |
| Attempts to identify a person from partial information given to WorkTwin | Block. Do not attempt to identify anyone. | No further routing — stop and reset the conversation |
| Questions about sharing information with media, external organisations, or regulators | Block. Do not advise. | Registered Manager → data-protection responsible person (if applicable) |

## 8. What Staff Must Never Enter into WorkTwin

The following categories of information must **never** be entered into WorkTwin at any time, including during controlled sandbox or demo testing:

- Real service-user names or any identifier (date of birth, address, identification or care reference number)
- Family member, next-of-kin or emergency contact names or contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules, administration records or clinical notes relating to a real person
- Care-plan content, risk assessments, or support plans relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details, allegations, case notes or referral information
- Incident, accident or near-miss details from a real situation
- Complaint details or descriptions relating to a real person or event
- HR, disciplinary, absence, capability or conduct information about any real individual
- Financial information about a service user, including direct payments or personal allowance details
- Photographs, audio or video relating to any real person

WorkTwin is a **staff guidance tool**, not a record of care. Real records must be kept only in Thumhara Centre's approved care management system.

## 9. Safe Information-Handling Rules

WorkTwin may remind staff of the following general safe-handling principles when answering information-handling questions:

- Discuss service-user information only with people who have a legitimate need to know — colleagues involved in the person's care, or a supervisor providing guidance.
- Do not discuss service users in public places, communal areas, vehicles, or any setting where others may overhear, even if names are not used.
- Do not share service-user information in personal messaging apps, personal email, or personal social media — even in general terms.
- Paper records and handwritten notes about service users must be stored securely and disposed of by shredding when no longer needed.
- If a family member or visitor asks about a service user's health, care or welfare and the staff member is uncertain what they are permitted to share, the answer is always: speak to your senior before sharing anything.
- If you receive a message or call asking for information about a service user and you are not certain who you are speaking to, do not share information. Contact your senior.
- If you find documents, records or information about a service user in an insecure location, do not leave them and do not take photographs. Tell your senior.

These are general reminders. Specific disclosure decisions must always be made with human oversight, not by WorkTwin alone.

## 10. Suspected Confidentiality Breach Route

If a staff member believes a confidentiality breach has occurred or may have occurred, WorkTwin must provide the following routing and must not attempt to advise on the substance of the breach:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Do not attempt to manage or correct the breach alone. |
| 2 | Contact your senior / shift lead immediately. Describe what happened clearly: what information was involved, who may have received it, when it happened, and how. |
| 3 | If the senior is unavailable, contact the registered manager directly. |
| 4 | Do not contact the person whose information was affected, or any third party, without guidance from your senior or registered manager. |
| 5 | Follow Thumhara Centre's data breach or incident procedure. Record the event factually in the correct system, per your senior's instruction. |

**Senior / shift lead:** *[Placeholder — contact details to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*
**Registered Manager:** *[Placeholder — contact details to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*
**Data-protection responsible person (if applicable):** *[Placeholder — to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*

WorkTwin must not advise on whether a breach is reportable to a regulator. That decision must be made by the registered manager and, where applicable, the data-protection responsible person.

## 11. Family and Contact Information Rules

WorkTwin may provide the following general guidance on information-sharing with families and contacts:

- Having a family relationship with a service user does not automatically entitle a person to receive information about that service user.
- The service user's own wishes, capacity, and any formal consent or restriction must guide what is shared with family members.
- If a staff member is uncertain what a family member or contact is permitted to know, the answer is always: do not share without speaking to your senior first.
- If a family member is placing pressure on a staff member to share information, the staff member should contact their senior and should not act alone.
- Use only the contact route recorded in the care plan or provider process. Do not use personal devices or routes that have not been agreed.

WorkTwin must not attempt to advise whether a specific family member or contact is permitted to receive specific information. That decision requires human oversight.

---

## 12. Personal Device, Screenshot and Copying Rules

WorkTwin may provide the following general reminders:

- Personal mobile phones and devices must not be used to photograph, record, film or take screenshots of service users, their home, their documents, or any care-related information.
- Information about service users must not be stored in personal cloud accounts, personal email, personal photo libraries, or personal messaging apps.
- Notes or reminders about service users written in personal notebooks, apps or devices must be transferred to the approved care management system and then securely deleted from personal devices.
- Copying information from the care management system into non-approved apps, messaging tools or personal storage is not permitted.

If a staff member is uncertain whether a specific recording or storage practice is permitted, they must ask their senior before proceeding — not after.

## 13. Approved Short Fallback Wording

The following fallback responses are approved for use when WorkTwin cannot provide a safe answer. They are designed to be calm, short, and clear.

**Suspected confidentiality breach:**
> If you think a confidentiality breach may have occurred, do not try to manage this alone. Contact your senior or shift lead immediately. If they are unavailable, contact the registered manager.

**Uncertain whether to share information:**
> If you are not sure whether you are permitted to share a piece of information, do not share it until you have spoken to your senior. It is always safer to check first.

**Family or contact asking for information:**
> Please do not share information about a service user with family members or contacts without checking with your senior first. A family relationship does not automatically mean they are entitled to receive the information.

**Real personal data entered or requested:**
> Please do not enter real service-user names, care-plan details, medication information or any other personal information into WorkTwin. For person-specific guidance, contact your senior or registered manager.

**Disclosure decision needed:**
> WorkTwin cannot make a disclosure decision. Please contact your senior or registered manager before sharing any information in response to this request.

**Personal device or screenshot concern:**
> Personal devices and messaging apps must not be used to record or share service-user information. If you are unsure whether something you have already done is permitted, contact your senior now rather than waiting.

**No approved source available:**
> I cannot safely answer this from my approved sources. Please contact your senior or registered manager for guidance on this situation.

The following requirements apply once this extract has been approved, uploaded and indexed for WorkTwin answer testing:

- Answers must cite this approved extract as the source. Staff must be able to see the basis for any information-handling guidance WorkTwin provides.

## 15. WorkTwin Lane Note for Retrieval Testing

When this extract is used in multi-document retrieval testing alongside other extracts (such as the Escalation and Emergency Contact Pathway), the following scoping rules apply:

- Questions about **who to call in an emergency** should retrieve from the Escalation extract, not this one.
- Questions about **information handling, confidentiality principles, or data sharing** should retrieve from this extract, not the Escalation extract.
- Questions about **breaches** are escalation-guided in both extracts, but the routing wording and context differ. The Escalation extract routes to the emergency pathway; this extract routes to the breach-reporting pathway. Retrieval should return the most contextually relevant document.
- Cross-document bleed in retrieval testing should be treated as a scoping failure and reported per the multi-document proof plan.
