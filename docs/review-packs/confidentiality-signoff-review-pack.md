## Section 1 — Cover Note: What WorkTwin Is and Is Not

### What WorkTwin is

WorkTwin is a staff guidance tool being developed as part of a care pilot with Thumhara Centre. It is designed to help care and support staff quickly find general guidance when they have a question about how to handle a situation — for example, reminders about confidentiality principles, what to do when uncertain, or who to contact when something needs to be escalated.

WorkTwin reads from a small set of approved policy documents. When a staff member asks a question, WorkTwin searches those documents and provides a brief, grounded response based only on what the approved document says. It always names the document it is drawing from.

### What WorkTwin is not

WorkTwin is **not**:

- A replacement for the registered manager, a senior, or any human colleague.
- A care record or care management system. No real care records, service-user information, or personal data should ever be entered into WorkTwin.
- A legal adviser, data-protection authority, or clinical decision-making tool.
- A system that can make decisions — about disclosure, about a service user, about whether a breach is reportable, or about anything that requires human judgement.
- Approved for live use. It is a pilot tool in a controlled sandbox environment. It is not live, and it is not available to staff at this stage.

WorkTwin is designed with one primary safety rule: if a question involves a real person, a sensitive decision, or anything that requires human judgement, WorkTwin stops advising and directs staff to a human — their senior, the registered manager, or another appropriate route.

### Where this policy fits

The Thumhara Centre Confidentiality and Information Handling Policy is one of the documents being prepared for WorkTwin to draw on. Before WorkTwin can use it, Thumhara Centre leadership and the registered manager must review it, confirm it is accurate, and approve it. That is what this review pack is for.

## Section 2 — What Lane B Means in Plain English

WorkTwin assigns each policy document a "lane" that defines how carefully it handles questions from that document. The Confidentiality and Information Handling Policy has been assigned **Lane B**.

**Lane B means: WorkTwin gives general guidance but stops and routes to a human for anything sensitive.**

In practice, this means:

- WorkTwin **can** remind staff of general principles — what confidentiality means, what kinds of information are confidential, what staff should not do with personal information, and what to do when uncertain.
- WorkTwin **will stop and direct staff to their senior or registered manager** if a question involves a suspected breach, an accidental disclosure, a request for information about a specific service user, a subject access request, or any situation that requires a real decision.
- WorkTwin **will block and refuse** any question that involves real service-user names, care-plan details, medication information, safeguarding details, incidents, complaints, or HR information — and will remind staff not to enter this kind of information.

Lane B was chosen for confidentiality because the consequences of incorrect guidance here are serious: a wrong answer about whether information may be shared could cause a reportable data breach or harm to a service user. WorkTwin is not equipped to make those calls and is deliberately designed not to try.

## Section 6 — No-Real-Data Warning

**This is a standing rule. It applies at all times, including during this review.**

The following must **never** be entered into WorkTwin or included in any document sent to or from WorkTwin:

- Real service-user names or any identifier (date of birth, address, care or identification reference number)
- Family member, next-of-kin or emergency contact names or contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules, administration records or clinical notes relating to a real person
- Care-plan content, risk assessments or support plans relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details, allegations, case notes or referral information
- Incident, accident or near-miss details from a real situation
- Complaint details or descriptions relating to a real person or event
- HR, disciplinary, absence, capability or conduct information about any real individual
- Financial information about a service user, including direct payments or personal allowance details
- Photographs, audio or video relating to any real person
- API keys, passwords, access credentials, or system configuration details

This pack contains no real personal data. The source policy and AI-safe extract contain no real personal data. All contact routes in both documents are role-based placeholders — they describe roles, not named individuals or real phone numbers. Real contact routes must be confirmed by Thumhara Centre and held separately in Thumhara Centre's own systems — not embedded in WorkTwin documents.
