# WorkTwin Product Brief

## Product category

Employee Success AI for regulated SMEs.

## One-line pitch

WorkTwin gives every employee a private AI work and learning companion that helps them onboard faster, understand policies, practise real workplace scenarios and grow in their role — without workplace surveillance.

## First version

WorkTwin Care Pilot.

## Target customer

UK care providers with 20–250 staff where managers are stretched, policies are hard to navigate, onboarding is inconsistent, and staff repeatedly ask the same operational/compliance questions.

## Core problem

Care providers already have the answers in policies, SOPs, handbooks and training documents, but staff often cannot find them, do not understand them, or feel embarrassed asking basic questions.

This creates:
- repeated manager interruptions
- inconsistent onboarding
- policy confusion
- avoidable process mistakes
- training gaps
- weaker audit confidence
- low new-starter confidence

## Solution

A private AI staff assistant that answers employee questions from approved company documents, explains policies in plain English, supports onboarding, creates short learning checks, and escalates sensitive issues to the correct human.

## Important trust rule

The employer does not see private employee chat transcripts by default.

The employer sees anonymised insights such as:
- common policy confusion areas
- repeated onboarding questions
- documents that need improving
- training topics staff practise most
- unanswered questions requiring new guidance
