# WorkTwin Care Demo Script

## Demo scenario

A new care worker logs in to WorkTwin during their first week.

## Demo question 1

Employee asks:

"What do I do if a service user refuses medication?"

WorkTwin answer format:

1. Plain-English answer
2. What to do next
3. Source
4. Escalate if
5. Learning option

Example response:

You should not force or pressure the service user. Record the refusal clearly, inform the senior member on duty, and follow the medication refusal procedure.

What to do next:
1. Stay calm and respect the person’s decision.
2. Record the refusal in the correct record.
3. Inform the senior member on duty.
4. Follow the escalation process if there is any risk.

Source:
Medication Policy, section 4.2: Refusal of Medication.

Escalate if:
The person’s health may be at immediate risk, the refusal is repeated, or you are unsure what to do.

Learning option:
Would you like to practise this with a short scenario?

## Demo question 2

"How do I report a safeguarding concern?"

Expected answer:
- Do not investigate yourself
- Record factual information
- Report immediately to the safeguarding lead/manager
- Follow the safeguarding policy
- Emergency risk requires immediate escalation

## Demo question 3

"Quiz me on incident reporting."

Expected output:
- 3 short scenario-based questions
- private feedback
- link back to incident reporting policy
