## 1. Purpose

This document creates the Thumhara-owned policy standard for WorkTwin-ready policies. It defines how Thumhara Centre policies must be structured, written and reviewed before they are used within the WorkTwin system.

**Critical rules that apply to all policies in this programme:**

- **QCS as benchmark only.** QCS policies may be used as a quality reference — to understand the regulatory standard expected for a given topic. They must not be treated as a source to copy from.
- **No copying of QCS wording.** Every Thumhara-owned policy must be written in entirely original wording. Reproducing QCS text, even in paraphrased or lightly adapted form, is not permitted.
- **Original authorship required.** Thumhara-owned policies must reflect Thumhara Centre's local practice, escalation contacts and operational context. They are not generic templates.
- **Leadership review before operational use.** No policy drafted under this programme may be used for live staff guidance or uploaded into WorkTwin until it has been reviewed and approved by Shagufta or the registered manager.
- **WorkTwin staff guidance must stay within approved policy boundaries.** Ask WorkTwin may only answer questions that fall within the scope of an approved, uploaded policy. It must not extrapolate beyond what the policy says.

---

## 2. Why Create Thumhara-Owned Policies

### 2.1 The limitation of QCS-style policies for WorkTwin

QCS policies are strong formal compliance documents. They are thorough, legally referenced and appropriate as a professional benchmark. However, they are designed for human readers working in a structured compliance environment. They are not designed to be indexed into an AI system and used for real-time staff guidance.

WorkTwin needs policies that are:

- **Clear.** Staff can understand the procedure without interpretation.
- **Local.** The escalation routes, contact names and thresholds reflect Thumhara Centre's own structure.
- **Plain-English.** Language is accessible to frontline care workers, not just managers.
- **AI-safe.** Each policy explicitly defines what WorkTwin may and may not answer, and when staff must escalate to a human.

### 2.2 Thumhara-owned policies can include things QCS cannot

A Thumhara-owned policy can state:

- The specific name or role of the escalation contact at Thumhara Centre (using a role placeholder until confirmed)
- The threshold at which WorkTwin should stop answering and direct staff to call the office or the registered manager
- The exact phrasing WorkTwin should use if asked a question outside policy scope
- Local context for food, culture, community communication or service-user preferences relevant to Thumhara's population

### 2.3 Copyright and licence risk

Copying or closely paraphrasing QCS content without a current QCS licence creates copyright and licence risk. Writing original policies eliminates this risk entirely and gives Thumhara Centre full ownership of its own documentation.

## 3. Thumhara Professional Policy Standard

Every policy written under this programme must follow this standard structure. Each heading is mandatory. Fields marked *placeholder* may use a role title until the named individual is confirmed by leadership.

---

### 3.1 Policy title

The full formal title of the policy. Must be specific enough to distinguish it from similar policies. Example: *Thumhara Centre Service User Refusal, Reassurance and Escalation Policy*.

---

### 3.2 Policy owner

The role responsible for maintaining this policy. Typically the registered manager or a nominated senior lead. Use a role title as a placeholder (e.g. *Registered Manager, Thumhara Centre*) until leadership confirms the named individual.

---

### 3.3 Version

Version number in the format `v1.0`, `v1.1`, etc. The first approved draft is `v1.0`. Minor updates that do not change procedure are `v1.1`, `v1.2`. Substantive procedural changes require a new minor or major version agreed with the policy owner.

---

### 3.4 Last reviewed

The date the policy was last formally reviewed. Format: DD Month YYYY. This field must be updated every time the policy is reviewed, even if no changes are made.

---

### 3.5 Next review date

The scheduled date for the next formal review. All Thumhara Centre policies under this programme must be reviewed at least annually. High-risk policies (Lane C) must be reviewed every six months.

---

### 3.6 Approved by

The name and role of the person who approved this version for operational use. No policy may be uploaded into WorkTwin or used for staff guidance until this field is completed. *Pending leadership sign-off* is acceptable as a placeholder in draft versions only.

---

### 3.7 Business impact rating

A rating reflecting the consequence if this policy is absent, ignored or poorly followed. Use one of:

- **Critical** — risk of harm to service users, staff or the public; regulatory breach likely
- **High** — significant operational or reputational risk
- **Medium** — internal process impact; recoverable
- **Low** — administrative only

---

### 3.8 Purpose

A short statement (two to four sentences) explaining what this policy is for and why it exists. Must answer: what does this policy govern, why does Thumhara Centre need it, and what harm does it prevent.

---

### 3.9 Scope

Who this policy applies to. State clearly which staff roles, settings and service-user groups are covered. State any explicit exclusions (e.g. *This policy does not cover medication administration decisions, which are governed by the Medication Policy*).

---

### 3.10 Legal and regulatory framework

List the legislation, regulations and standards that underpin this policy. Examples relevant to domiciliary care include:

- Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014
- Care Act 2014
- Mental Capacity Act 2005
- Human Rights Act 1998
- CQC Fundamental Standards
- Relevant NICE guidelines (where applicable)

Do not fabricate references. Only include legislation that genuinely applies to the policy topic.

---

### 3.11 Underpinning knowledge

List the key knowledge, skills or standards a staff member needs in order to follow this policy correctly. This section supports training planning and competency assessment. Examples: *knowledge of service user rights*, *awareness of capacity assessment principles*, *understanding of escalation thresholds*.

---

### 3.12 Policy statement

A clear, plain-English statement of Thumhara Centre's commitment and position on this topic. This is not a procedure — it is a statement of values and intent. It should be short (four to eight sentences) and written in a tone accessible to frontline staff.

---

### 3.13 Procedure

The step-by-step procedure staff must follow. This is the core operational section. It must be:

- Numbered and sequential
- Written in plain English
- Specific enough that a new care worker could follow it without interpretation
- Free of jargon unless the term is explained
- Linked to escalation thresholds (e.g. *If the service user has not accepted food or fluids within [threshold], contact the office immediately*)

---

### 3.14 Roles and responsibilities

A table or bulleted list stating which role is responsible for which action within the policy. Minimum roles to address: Care Worker, Senior Care Worker / Team Leader, Registered Manager, Office / Coordinator. Add other roles where relevant (e.g. Medication Lead, Safeguarding Lead).

---

### 3.15 Records and reporting

What must be recorded, where it must be recorded, and when. State the existing care-recording system (e.g. *Care records must be entered in [Thumhara's care management system] at the end of each visit*). WorkTwin is not a record of care — this section must make clear that real events are recorded in the existing system, not in WorkTwin.

---

### 3.16 Escalation route

The specific escalation pathway for this policy. Must include:

- The immediate action (what the care worker does first)
- Who to contact (role title as placeholder until name confirmed)
- Contact method (phone call, not message, for urgent matters)
- What to do if the first escalation contact is unavailable
- When to call 999 directly without waiting for an internal escalation response

---

### 3.17 Training and competency

How staff are trained on this policy, how competency is assessed, and how often. Include:

- Induction training requirement (yes/no)
- Refresher frequency
- Competency check method (observation, written confirmation, discussion)
- Record of training completion (where stored)

---

### 3.18 Monitoring and audit

How compliance with this policy is monitored. Include:

- What is monitored (e.g. care records reviewed, incidents logged)
- Who monitors it
- How often
- What happens if non-compliance is found

---

### 3.19 Equality, dignity and human rights

A statement confirming that this policy is applied consistently regardless of age, disability, gender, ethnicity, religion or other protected characteristic. Note any specific equality considerations relevant to this policy's topic. For Thumhara Centre's population, consider cultural, linguistic and communication factors where relevant.

---

### 3.20 Related policies

A list of other Thumhara Centre policies that staff should also be aware of when applying this policy. Use policy titles, not external references. Example: *See also: Escalation and Emergency Contact Pathway Policy; Confidentiality and Information Handling Policy*.

---

### 3.21 WorkTwin AI-use note

A mandatory section in every policy. See Section 4 of this document for the full definition of what this section must contain.

---

### 3.22 Review log

A table recording every formal review of this policy, whether or not changes were made:

| Date | Reviewed by | Changes made | Next review due |
|------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| DD Month YYYY | Role title | Brief summary or "No changes" | DD Month YYYY |

## 4. WorkTwin AI-Use Rules Section

Every Thumhara-owned policy must include a *WorkTwin AI-use note* section. This section must define the following points in plain English.

### 4.1 What WorkTwin may answer

List the specific types of question Ask WorkTwin is permitted to answer using this policy as a source. Be specific. Example: *WorkTwin may answer questions about the general procedure for responding to a service user who refuses personal care, and about what to say to a distressed service user to offer reassurance.*

### 4.2 What WorkTwin must not answer

List the types of question Ask WorkTwin must not attempt to answer, even if the policy contains related content. Examples:

- Medication decisions of any kind
- Whether a specific service user has capacity to refuse
- Whether a safeguarding concern should be raised in a specific situation
- Emergency clinical assessments
- Advice that requires knowledge of an individual service user's care plan

### 4.3 When WorkTwin must escalate to a human lead

Define the specific circumstances in which WorkTwin must stop answering and direct the staff member to call a human immediately. Examples:

- The staff member indicates the situation has become an emergency
- The question involves a specific named service user and a safety concern
- The staff member asks whether to call 999
- The question cannot be answered from the uploaded policy alone

### 4.4 What staff must never enter into WorkTwin

Remind staff that the following must never be entered into Ask WorkTwin:

- Full names of service users
- Addresses or location details of service users
- Medication names, doses or schedules
- Family or next-of-kin contact details
- Specific incident details from an ongoing case
- Any information that would identify a service user to a third party

### 4.5 What WorkTwin does not replace

Every policy's AI-use note must include the following reminder:

> WorkTwin does not replace your senior care worker, registered manager, medication lead, safeguarding lead or emergency services. If you are unsure what to do, call your supervisor. If there is an immediate risk to life, call 999.

### 4.6 Where real records must be kept

Every policy's AI-use note must remind staff:

> Real care events, incidents and observations must be recorded in Thumhara Centre's care management system, not in WorkTwin. WorkTwin is a staff guidance tool, not a record of care.

## 5. Policy Lane Model

Every Thumhara-owned policy is assigned to a lane. The lane determines how that policy may be used within WorkTwin and what Ask WorkTwin may do with it.

---

### Lane A — Safe for Ask answers

**What belongs here:** Policies covering general procedure, professional conduct, record-keeping and operational process where the guidance is practical, bounded and does not require clinical judgement or knowledge of individual service users.

**Example questions Ask WorkTwin can answer:**
- *What should I do if a service user refuses their lunch?*
- *What should I say to a service user who is upset and wants to stay in bed?*
- *How do I record a late visit?*

**Ask WorkTwin behaviour:** May provide a full procedural answer drawing on the uploaded policy. Must include an escalation reminder if the situation sounds urgent.

**Escalation behaviour:** Reminds staff to call the office or registered manager if the situation is beyond the standard procedure.

**Upload and readiness status:** Policy reviewed and approved by leadership. Safe to upload and index for Ask responses.

---

### Lane B — Cautious staff-visible support

**What belongs here:** Policies covering topics where the guidance is useful but where individual circumstances may change the correct course of action, and where a wrong answer could cause harm. Staff can see the policy and Ask WorkTwin can answer cautiously, but must consistently prompt escalation.

**Example questions Ask WorkTwin can answer:**
- *A service user has not eaten or drunk anything since my last visit. What should I do?*
- *A family member is at the door and I am not sure if they are on the approved contact list. What should I do?*

**Ask WorkTwin behaviour:** Provides general procedural guidance but always includes a prompt to escalate. Must not attempt to answer for specific service users.

**Escalation behaviour:** Consistently directs staff to call the office or registered manager rather than making a unilateral decision.

**Upload and readiness status:** Policy reviewed and approved by leadership. Requires a carefully written AI-use note that clearly defines escalation thresholds before upload.

---

### Lane C — Escalation-only / human-led

**What belongs here:** Policies covering topics where the risk of harm from incorrect AI guidance is too high for WorkTwin to provide procedural answers. WorkTwin may acknowledge the topic, remind staff of who to contact, and provide a calming response — nothing more.

**Example questions that trigger escalation-only:**
- *Should I give the service user their medication if they are refusing?*
- *I think this might be a safeguarding situation. What do I do?*
- *The service user has fallen and I am not sure if they are hurt.*

**Ask WorkTwin behaviour:** Does not attempt to answer the procedural question. Acknowledges the concern, directs staff to call 999 if there is immediate risk to life, or to call the office and registered manager immediately for all other Lane C situations.

**Escalation behaviour:** Mandatory and immediate. WorkTwin must not provide interim guidance before the escalation prompt.

**Upload and readiness status:** Policy may be uploaded for staff reference only. WorkTwin is configured not to answer questions in this lane. Requires explicit configuration before indexing.

---

### Lane D — Do not upload / do not use yet

**What belongs here:** Policies that have not yet been reviewed and approved by Thumhara leadership, policies that contain real service-user data, policies that are QCS copies, and policies covering topics the project has not yet assessed for AI safety.

**Example policies in this lane now:**
- Any policy not yet drafted under this programme
- Any QCS policy printed or copied from the QCS system
- Any document containing real service-user names, medication details or case notes

**Ask WorkTwin behaviour:** Not applicable. These policies are not uploaded.

**Escalation behaviour:** Not applicable.

**Upload and readiness status:** Not ready. Must not be uploaded until the policy has been written, reviewed, approved and assigned to Lane A, B or C.

## 7. First Policy to Draft

**Recommended starting point:**

> Thumhara Centre Service User Refusal, Reassurance and Escalation Policy

### 7.1 Why this policy first

This policy is the highest priority for the following reasons:

- **It directly reflects Shagufta's feedback.** During the controlled preview session, Shagufta identified refusal situations — food refusal, personal care refusal, toilet refusal — as a core area where care workers need clear, confident guidance. This policy addresses that gap directly.
- **It supports realistic care-worker situations.** Refusal is one of the most common and most challenging situations frontline care workers face. A clear, approved policy gives them the confidence to respond well.
- **It is useful for trusted staff practice.** Ask WorkTwin can be tested with realistic refusal scenarios without requiring real service-user data. The policy provides the boundary for what Ask may and may not say.
- **It can be written safely as guidance and escalation support.** The policy focuses on communication, reassurance and escalation. It does not require clinical judgement, medication decisions or safeguarding assessment. It is within safe scope for Lane A and Lane B use.
- **It links naturally to related topics.** Food refusal, personal care refusal, toilet refusal, refusal to come inside (e.g. refusing to let the care worker in), and delayed family or contact response are all connected. A single well-structured policy can address all of these as sub-procedures or linked guidance.
- **It does not require real service-user profiles.** The policy can be tested using the fictional service-user profiles already created for WorkTwin. No real confidential data is needed.

## 8. Drafting Rules for the First Policy

The first policy — and every subsequent policy in this programme — must follow these non-negotiable drafting rules:

- **Use original wording.** No sentence may be copied or closely paraphrased from QCS or any other third-party policy source.
- **Write in plain English but maintain a professional tone.** The language must be clear enough for a new care worker to follow without a manager present, but it must be consistent with a professional care organisation's standards.
- **Include local escalation route placeholders.** Where a specific name, phone number or contact route is needed, use a clearly marked placeholder (e.g. *[Thumhara Centre Office — contact details to be confirmed]*). Do not invent specific contact details.
- **Avoid medication decision-making.** The policy must not advise staff on whether or how to administer, withhold or adjust medication. Medication decisions belong in the Medication Policy and must be led by the medication lead or registered manager.
- **Avoid safeguarding case advice.** If a refusal situation gives rise to a safeguarding concern, the policy must direct staff to the registered manager immediately. The policy must not attempt to guide staff through a safeguarding assessment.
- **Avoid emergency decision-making beyond 999 and escalation.** If there is an immediate risk to life, the policy must direct staff to call 999. The policy must not attempt to guide staff through emergency clinical assessment.
- **Tell staff to record real events in the existing care system.** Every procedural step that involves recording must state clearly that the record goes into Thumhara Centre's care management system, not into WorkTwin.
- **Include a WorkTwin AI-use note.** The completed policy must include a WorkTwin AI-use note following the standard defined in Section 4 of this document.
- **Be reviewed by Shagufta or the registered manager before upload or use.** The draft may be shared with the WorkTwin project team for internal review, but it must not be used for live staff guidance or uploaded into WorkTwin until Thumhara leadership has reviewed and approved it.

## 9. Policy Quality Checklist

Before any Thumhara-owned policy is submitted for leadership review, it must pass all items on this checklist. Every item must be confirmed.

- [ ] Written in entirely original wording — no copied or closely paraphrased QCS text
- [ ] Clear purpose statement that explains what the policy governs and why it exists
- [ ] Staff-friendly procedure — numbered, sequential, plain English, actionable
- [ ] Legal and regulatory references included and correct for the topic
- [ ] Local escalation contact route included (role placeholders acceptable; no invented names or numbers)
- [ ] Escalation thresholds defined (e.g. what triggers a call to the office, what triggers a call to 999)
- [ ] Records and reporting section specifies the existing care-recording system
- [ ] WorkTwin AI-use note included, covering all six required points from Section 4
- [ ] Safe and unsafe questions listed within the AI-use note
- [ ] Policy lane assigned (A, B, C or D) and agreed by the WorkTwin project team
- [ ] Reviewed and approved by Shagufta or the registered manager before upload
- [ ] No real service-user names, addresses, medication details, family contacts or confidential case data included in the document
- [ ] No copied QCS wording — confirmed by the policy author
