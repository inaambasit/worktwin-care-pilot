# Customer Discovery Questions

Speak to 20 potential buyers before building too much.

## Target people

- care provider owners
- registered managers
- operations managers
- HR managers
- compliance leads
- training providers
- consultants who support care providers

## Questions

1. How do new starters currently learn your policies and procedures?
2. Where are your staff handbook, SOPs and training documents stored?
3. What questions do staff ask repeatedly?
4. Which policies cause the most confusion?
5. How much time do managers spend answering basic process questions?
6. What mistakes happen when staff do not understand the process?
7. How do you currently evidence that staff understand policies?
8. Would a private AI assistant for staff be useful if it only answered from approved documents?
9. What would make you worried about using AI with staff?
10. Would staff trust it if their private chats were not visible to management?
11. What anonymised insights would be useful for managers?
12. Would you pay for a 60–90 day pilot?
13. What would make this worth £500–£1,000/month?
14. Who would need to approve it?
15. What documents would you want to upload first?

## Validation target

Proceed only when:
- at least 10 buyers confirm the pain is real
- at least 5 ask to see a demo
- at least 2–3 are willing to discuss a paid pilot
