# Lovable / v0 Prototype Prompt

Build a modern SaaS prototype called WorkTwin Care Pilot.

WorkTwin is a private AI onboarding, policy and learning assistant for care providers.

Design a clean, professional web app with two user areas:

1. Employee dashboard
2. Admin dashboard

Use a calm, trustworthy B2B SaaS style. Make it feel safe, professional and privacy-first, not flashy.

## Employee dashboard pages

### Ask WorkTwin
A chat interface where a care worker can ask questions about company policies and procedures.

Suggested prompts:
- What do I do if a service user refuses medication?
- How do I report a safeguarding concern?
- What happens if I am off sick?
- What should I focus on in my first week?
- Quiz me on incident reporting.
- Help me prepare for my probation review.

Show a sample answer card with:
- Plain-English answer
- What to do next
- Source document
- Escalate if
- Button: Practise this scenario
- Button: Save to private notes

### My Onboarding
Show a checklist:
- Read staff handbook
- Understand safeguarding policy
- Complete medication process review
- Learn incident reporting
- Prepare first supervision questions

### Practice Scenarios
Show cards:
- Medication refusal
- Safeguarding concern
- Family complaint
- Incident reporting
- Sickness reporting

### Private Notes
A private area where the employee can save development notes and questions for their manager.

Make it clear:
"Your private notes are not shared with management unless you choose to share them."

## Admin dashboard pages

### Company Documents
Upload area for:
- Staff handbook
- Safeguarding policy
- Medication policy
- Incident reporting procedure
- Sickness policy
- Complaints process
- Onboarding pack

### Anonymous Insights
Show dashboard cards:
- Top staff question areas
- Policy confusion trends
- Unanswered questions
- Training gaps
- Suggested document improvements

Important message:
"Managers do not see private employee chat transcripts. Insights are anonymised."

### Roles and Permissions
Let admin assign documents to:
- Care worker
- Senior care worker
- Admin
- Manager

### Escalation Contacts
Let admin set:
- Safeguarding lead
- HR contact
- Registered manager
- Medication lead
