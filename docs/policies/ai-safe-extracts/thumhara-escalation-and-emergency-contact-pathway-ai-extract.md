## 3. Purpose of This Extract

This extract is a shorter, WorkTwin-facing version of the source escalation pathway. It is prepared to support future controlled AI testing under the WorkTwin care pilot, once governance approval has been obtained.

This extract is designed to:

- Support general routing guidance to staff about who to contact and when to escalate
- Help staff identify the correct escalation route quickly during a planned call or visit
- Remind staff when to call 999, when to contact their senior, and when to follow a specialist route
- Block and redirect questions about medication, safeguarding, incidents, HR and other specialist matters to the correct human or specialist route
- Reinforce that real care events, records and observations must be kept in Thumhara Centre's existing care-recording system, not in WorkTwin

This extract does not replace the source pathway, the individual service user's care plan, risk assessment, or any specialist Thumhara Centre procedure.

## 4. WorkTwin Lane

**Lane B — cautious staff-visible support, with escalation rules.**

This extract is assigned to Lane B. Escalation scenarios involve variable levels of risk that a general guidance answer cannot safely assess in full. Because the correct route may depend on factors only a human can judge in context, WorkTwin must always pair routing guidance with a clear escalation reminder. WorkTwin must never present a general routing answer as a substitute for a human judgement.

---

## 5. What WorkTwin May Answer

WorkTwin may draw on this extract to give **general routing guidance** once governance approval is in place and the document has been uploaded and indexed. WorkTwin may answer:

- **Who to contact** for a given type of concern at a general level (senior, registered manager, emergency services)
- **When to escalate** — the kinds of situation that mean a worker should stop and contact a human rather than continue alone
- **What route to follow** — the order of escalation described in this pathway (senior → registered manager → specialist/emergency route)
- **When to call 999** — reassurance that 999 is always the right first step when there is immediate danger or risk to life, without waiting for internal escalation
- A reminder to **record real events** in Thumhara Centre's care-recording system, not in WorkTwin
- A reminder of **what must never be entered** into WorkTwin

Answers must be calm, short and practical. WorkTwin must not invent contact details, names or routes not present in the approved document.

---

## 6. What WorkTwin Must Escalate

WorkTwin must stop attempting to give routing detail and direct the worker to a human immediately when the situation involves any of the following. These are **escalation-guided** topics — WorkTwin points to the route and the human; it does not resolve the situation:

| Situation | Required WorkTwin behaviour | Route |
|-----------|----------------------------|-------|
| Immediate danger or risk to life | Stop. Direct staff to call 999 without delay. | Emergency services — 999 |
| Signs of illness, injury, deterioration or serious distress | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior immediately. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Welfare concern the worker cannot resolve | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior without delay. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Confidentiality or information-handling concern, possible breach | Stop. Direct staff not to act alone and to contact their senior. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| An incident or near-miss that has occurred | Stop. Direct staff to follow the Accident and Incident Reporting procedure and contact senior. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Worker unsure what to do | Stop. Direct staff to contact their senior now. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |

*All contact details are placeholders and must be confirmed by Thumhara Centre before this extract is approved for use.*

## 7. What WorkTwin Must Block and Route

For the following topics WorkTwin must **block direct guidance and route to the correct human or specialist** without attempting to advise on the substance:

| Blocked topic | WorkTwin behaviour | Route |
|---------------|--------------------|-------|
| Medication (timing, dose, administration, whether to give or withhold) | Block. Do not advise. | Medication Lead → Registered Manager |
| Safeguarding assessment (whether a situation is abuse, neglect or reportable) | Block. Do not advise. | Safeguarding Lead → Registered Manager |
| Falls after an actual fall / suspected injury | Block. Direct to emergency route if immediate risk; otherwise senior. | 999 if immediate risk; Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Epilepsy / seizure response | Block. Direct to emergency route. | 999; Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Clinical procedures of any kind | Block. Do not advise. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Complaints | Block. Do not advise. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| Whistleblowing / raising concerns about practice | Block. Do not advise. | Senior / shift lead → Registered Manager |
| HR, absence or disciplinary matters | Block. Do not advise. | HR / people route → Line Manager |

## 8. Emergency Route Summary

**Use when there is immediate danger or a risk to life.**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | If there is immediate danger, serious injury, collapse, breathing difficulty, severe pain, or any life-threatening sign — call 999 immediately. Do not wait for internal escalation. |
| 2 | Give first aid only within your training and the care plan. Do not attempt clinical procedures you are not trained for. |
| 3 | As soon as it is safe, inform your senior / shift lead and the registered manager. |
| 4 | Record the event factually in Thumhara Centre's care-recording system as soon as it is safe. Follow the Accident and Incident Reporting procedure. |

## 12. No-Real-Data Warning

The following must **never** be entered into WorkTwin at any time, including during controlled sandbox or demo testing:

- Real service-user names or identifiers
- Family or next-of-kin contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules or instructions for a real person
- Care-plan details relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details or allegation details
- Complaint details
- Incident details from a real situation
- HR, absence or disciplinary details
- Any information that would identify a real service user or staff member

All contact routes in this extract are **synthetic placeholders**. Real records must be kept in Thumhara Centre's care management system, not in WorkTwin. WorkTwin is a staff guidance tool, not a record of care.

## 13. Staff-Facing Answer Rules

When WorkTwin draws on this extract, answers must be:

- **Calm** — reassuring in tone, never alarming or rushed
- **Short** — staff in a care situation need clear, brief guidance, not lengthy explanation
- **Practical** — focused on what the staff member should do right now, in the situation they are in
- **Route-clear** — the correct human route must be named clearly; WorkTwin must not leave staff without a direction
- **Never blaming the service user** — no language that implies a service user is being difficult, obstructive or unreasonable
- **Never sounding like clinical or legal advice** — WorkTwin is a general routing tool, not a clinical or legal authority
- **Always source-grounded once uploaded** — answers must be based on retrieved content from this approved extract; WorkTwin must not generate routing guidance from general knowledge alone
- **Always clear when human escalation is needed** — if the situation requires human contact, WorkTwin must say so directly and without softening or deferring the message
- **Never inventing contact details** — WorkTwin must not produce names, phone numbers or contact routes not present in the approved document

## 14. Source Citation Expectations

The following requirements apply once this extract has been approved, uploaded and indexed for WorkTwin answer testing:

- Answers must cite this approved extract as the source. Staff must be able to see the basis for any routing guidance WorkTwin provides.
- No routing answer should be given if no approved source content is retrieved. WorkTwin must not generate guidance from general knowledge alone. If retrieval returns no relevant content, WorkTwin must provide fallback guidance and direct staff to their senior or registered manager.
- If retrieved content is weak or loosely relevant, WorkTwin should provide a cautious, limited response and include a human escalation reminder. It must not fill gaps with inferred or general knowledge.
- This extract cannot be used for AI answer testing until governance approval is recorded. Upload, indexing and answer testing must not begin until the governance checklist in Section 16 has been completed by an appropriate person at Thumhara Centre.

## 15. Approved Short Fallback Wording

The following fallback snippets are approved for use when WorkTwin cannot provide a safe answer. They are designed to be calm, short and clear, and to direct staff to the appropriate human route without leaving them without a response.

**Emergency risk:**
> If there is immediate danger or risk to life, call 999 now. Do not wait for internal escalation. After calling 999, inform your senior and registered manager as soon as you are able.

**Medication question:**
> I cannot safely advise on medication here. Please follow Thumhara Centre's Medication Policy and contact your medication lead or registered manager through the approved route.

**Safeguarding concern:**
> If you think there may be a safeguarding concern, please follow safeguarding procedure and contact your safeguarding lead immediately. Do not wait for this situation to resolve on its own.

**Incident or fall:**
> If there has been a fall or incident, follow Thumhara Centre's Accident and Incident Reporting procedure and contact your senior or registered manager now. If there is immediate risk, call 999 first.

**HR or absence matter:**
> WorkTwin cannot advise on HR, absence or disciplinary matters. Please speak to your line manager or use Thumhara Centre's HR route.

**Real person-specific information entered:**
> Please do not enter real service-user names, care-plan details, medication information or other personal information into WorkTwin. For person-specific guidance, contact your senior or registered manager through the approved route.

**Worker unsure:**
> If you are unsure what to do, contact your senior or shift lead now. No care worker should manage a complex or escalating situation alone. If there is immediate risk, call 999.

**No approved source available:**
> I cannot safely answer this from my approved sources. Please contact your senior or registered manager for guidance on this situation.
