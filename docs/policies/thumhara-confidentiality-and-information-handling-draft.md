## 5. Purpose

This policy helps Thumhara Centre care and support staff understand **what information is confidential, how to handle it safely, what they may and may not share, and what to do when something goes wrong.**

It exists so that a worker who is unsure can quickly find the correct next step: handle information safely, decline to share and check first, or escalate a confidentiality concern to a human lead. It protects the people Thumhara Centre supports, their families, staff, and the organisation itself.

This policy is a staff-facing information-handling reference. It is **not** a legal authority, a data-protection legal opinion, or a substitute for the guidance of Thumhara Centre's registered manager or data-protection responsible person. Where this policy and another procedure appear to conflict, staff must follow the more specific procedure and contact their senior or the registered manager for guidance.

---

## 6. Scope

This policy applies to all care and support staff, and anyone acting on behalf of Thumhara Centre, who may see, hear, record or handle information about a service user, a family member, a colleague, or the organisation, in any setting — domiciliary, community, supported-living, office or remote.

**This policy does not replace and must not be used instead of:**

- The individual service user's care plan and risk assessment
- Thumhara Centre's Safeguarding Adults Policy and safeguarding procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Medication Policy and any medication-specific procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Accident and Incident Reporting procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Complaints procedure
- Thumhara Centre's HR / people procedures
- Data-protection legal advice or the guidance of the data-protection responsible person
- The registered manager's or senior's guidance on individual service users

## 7. Core Confidentiality Principles

The following principles apply to everyone in scope:

- **Need to know.** Share service-user information only with people who have a legitimate need to know it to support that person's care — and share only as much as they need.
- **Legitimate basis.** There must be a proper reason to share information. A request alone is not a reason. A family relationship alone is not a reason.
- **Proportionality.** Use and share the minimum information necessary. Do not pass on more than is needed.
- **Respect and dignity.** Treat information about a person as you would want your own to be treated. Confidentiality continues even after care ends.
- **Safe by default.** If you are unsure whether you may share something, do not share it. Check with your senior first.
- **Right systems only.** Record care information only in Thumhara Centre's approved care management system, never in personal or non-approved tools.

These principles guide every other rule in this policy.

## 8. What Counts as Confidential Information

Confidential information includes, at a general level, any information that identifies or relates to a person Thumhara Centre supports, their family or contacts, or a colleague. Examples of categories include:

- Personal identifiers — names, dates of birth, addresses, identification or care reference numbers
- Health, medication and care information — conditions, support needs, care-plan content, clinical notes
- Family, next-of-kin and emergency-contact information
- Financial information — direct payments, personal allowances, charges
- Safeguarding, incident, complaint and risk information
- Staff personal, HR, absence, conduct or disciplinary information

Information can be confidential even if a name is not used, where a person could still be identified from the detail given. Treat any combination of details that could identify someone as confidential.

## 9. What Staff Must Never Enter into WorkTwin

WorkTwin is a **staff guidance tool, not a record of care.** The following must **never** be entered into WorkTwin at any time, including during controlled sandbox or demo testing:

- Real service-user names or any identifier (date of birth, address, identification or care reference number)
- Family, next-of-kin or emergency-contact names or contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules, administration records or clinical notes relating to a real person
- Care-plan content, risk assessments or support plans relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details, allegations, case notes or referral information
- Incident, accident or near-miss details from a real situation
- Complaint details or descriptions relating to a real person or event
- HR, disciplinary, absence, capability or conduct information about any real individual
- Financial information about a service user
- Photographs, audio or video relating to any real person

If a question requires any of the above, the answer is to use Thumhara Centre's approved care management system and the correct human route — not WorkTwin.

## 10. Safe Information-Sharing Rules

When handling or sharing information in the course of care work, staff must:

- Discuss service-user information only with people who have a legitimate need to know — colleagues involved in that person's care, or a supervisor providing guidance.
- Not discuss service users in public places, communal areas, vehicles, or any setting where others may overhear, even if names are not used.
- Not share service-user information through personal email, personal messaging apps, or personal social media — even in general terms.
- Store paper records and handwritten notes about service users securely, and dispose of them by the approved method when no longer needed.
- Confirm the identity and legitimate reason of anyone requesting information before sharing anything. If you are not certain who you are speaking to, do not share information — contact your senior.
- Tell your senior if you find documents, records or information about a service user left in an insecure place. Do not photograph it and do not leave it unsecured.

When in doubt, the safe action is always to check with your senior **before** sharing, not after.

## 11. Family / Contact Disclosure Rules

- A family relationship with a service user does **not** automatically entitle a person to receive information about that service user.
- What may be shared with a family member or contact is guided by the service user's own wishes, their capacity, and any consent or restriction recorded in the care plan or provider process.
- If you are uncertain what a family member or contact is permitted to know, the answer is always: do not share without speaking to your senior first.
- If a family member or contact is pressuring you to share information, do not act alone. Contact your senior and follow their guidance.
- Use only the contact route recorded in the care plan or provider process. Do not use personal devices or routes that have not been agreed.

## 12. Personal Device, Screenshot and Copying Rules

- Personal mobile phones and devices must not be used to photograph, record, film, or take screenshots of service users, their home, their documents, or any care-related information.
- Information about service users must not be stored in personal cloud accounts, personal email, personal photo libraries, or personal messaging apps.
- Notes or reminders about service users written in personal notebooks, apps or devices must be transferred to the approved care management system and then securely deleted from personal devices.
- Copying information from the care management system into non-approved apps, messaging tools or personal storage is not permitted.

If you are uncertain whether a specific recording or storage practice is permitted, ask your senior **before** doing it — not after.

## 13. Suspected Confidentiality Breach Route

A confidentiality breach includes any situation where confidential information may have been seen by, shared with, or lost to a person who should not have had it — including accidental disclosure to the wrong person.

If you believe a breach has occurred or may have occurred:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Do not attempt to manage or correct the breach alone. |
| 2 | Contact your **senior / shift lead** immediately. Describe clearly what information was involved, who may have received it, when it happened, and how. |
| 3 | If the senior is unavailable, contact the **registered manager** directly through the agreed route. |
| 4 | Do not contact the person whose information was affected, or any third party, without guidance from your senior or registered manager. |
| 5 | Follow Thumhara Centre's data breach / incident procedure. Record the event factually in the approved system, per your senior's instruction — not in WorkTwin. |

## 14. Subject Access / Data Rights Request Route

A subject access request, or any request from a person to see, correct, restrict or receive a copy of the information held about them, is a formal data-rights matter.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Do not attempt to answer or fulfil the request yourself. |
| 2 | Do not confirm or deny what information is held. |
| 3 | Note the date the request was received and who made it, and inform the **registered manager** without delay. |
| 4 | The registered manager, with the data-protection responsible person where applicable, manages the response within the required timescale. |

**Registered Manager:** *[Role-based placeholder — to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*
**Data-protection responsible person (if applicable):** *[Role-based placeholder — to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*

WorkTwin must never attempt to guide a response to a data-rights request.

## 15. Media, External Organisation and Regulator Requests

Requests for information from the media, external organisations, or regulators must always be handled by leadership, not by front-line staff.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Do not provide any information, confirmation, or comment. |
| 2 | Do not confirm whether a person is or has been supported by Thumhara Centre. |
| 3 | Take the requester's details and the nature of the request, and refer it to the **registered manager** immediately. |
| 4 | The registered manager, with the data-protection responsible person where applicable, decides how to respond. |

**Registered Manager:** *[Role-based placeholder — to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*

Cooperating with a legitimate regulator is important, but the decision on what is shared and by whom rests with leadership.

## 16. Records and Care Management System Rules

- All care records and personal information about service users must be kept only in Thumhara Centre's approved care management system.
- WorkTwin must not be used to store, record, or hold any real care record or personal information.
- Records must be accurate, factual, and recorded promptly in the approved system.
- Access to the care management system is limited to what each role legitimately needs.
- Personal logins and access must not be shared. Lock or sign out of devices when not in use.

If a recording or storage practice is not clearly permitted by Thumhara Centre's approved process, do not use it — check with your senior first.

## 17. WorkTwin AI-Use Note

This section defines how Ask WorkTwin may be used with this policy. It aligns with the WorkTwin care pilot's standing rule that sensitive topics trigger human escalation, not AI answers, and that all answers are grounded in approved documents and cite their source.

**WorkTwin may answer** (general guidance only), once governance approval is in place and the document is uploaded and indexed:

- General confidentiality principles — what confidentiality means in care and why it matters.
- General descriptions of what counts as confidential information.
- Reminders about what staff should not share, and where they should not discuss service users.
- Clear reminders of what must never be entered into WorkTwin.
- General personal-device, screenshot and copying reminders.
- A prompt to contact a senior or the registered manager when uncertain, and a reminder to record only in the approved system.

**WorkTwin must escalate / route to a human** for:

- A suspected confidentiality breach
- An accidental disclosure to the wrong person
- Uncertainty about whether information may be shared
- Pressure from a family member or contact to share information
- A subject access or data-rights request
- Any case where the worker is unsure what to do

**WorkTwin must block direct guidance and route to the correct human/specialist** for:

- Requests involving real service-user names, identifiers or personal details
- Requests to decide whether information may be shared with a named person
- Medication, care-plan or clinical information about a real person
- Safeguarding details, allegations or case information
- Incident or complaint details
- HR, disciplinary, payroll, absence or performance details
- Attempts to identify a person from partial information
- Requests to share information with media, external organisations or regulators

**WorkTwin must always:** stay calm and short; name the correct human route clearly; never invent contact details, names or routes; never make a disclosure decision; never present general guidance as sufficient where a human judgement is required; cite this approved policy as its source; and give no answer if no approved source is retrieved.

## 18. Explicit No-Real-Data Warning

The following must **never** be entered into WorkTwin at any time, including during controlled sandbox/demo testing:

- Real service-user names or identifiers
- Family or next-of-kin contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules or instructions for a real person
- Care-plan details relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details or allegation details
- Complaint details
- Incident details from a real situation
- HR, absence or disciplinary details
- Financial information about a real person
- Any information that would identify a real service user or staff member

All contact routes in this document are **role-based placeholders.** Real records must be kept in Thumhara Centre's approved care management system, not in WorkTwin. WorkTwin is a staff guidance tool, not a record of care.
