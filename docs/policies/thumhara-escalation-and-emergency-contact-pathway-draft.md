## 5. Purpose

This pathway helps Thumhara Centre care and support staff know **who to contact, when to escalate, and which route to follow** when a situation goes beyond routine support during a planned call or visit.

It exists so that a worker who is unsure can quickly find the correct next step: continue, escalate to a senior, escalate to the registered manager, follow a specialist route (safeguarding, medication), or call emergency services. It is a routing aid, not a clinical, medical or legal authority.

This pathway does not replace a service user's individual care plan, risk assessment, or any specialist Thumhara Centre procedure. Where this pathway and another procedure appear to conflict, staff must follow the more specific procedure and contact their senior or the registered manager for guidance.

---

## 6. Scope

This pathway applies to all care and support staff delivering care or support on behalf of Thumhara Centre, in domiciliary, community or supported-living settings, during any planned call or visit.

**This pathway does not replace and must not be used instead of:**

- The individual service user's care plan and risk assessment
- Thumhara Centre's Medication Policy and any medication-specific procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Safeguarding Adults Policy and safeguarding procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Accident and Incident Reporting procedure
- Thumhara Centre's Emergency Procedure
- Clinical judgement from a qualified health or medical professional
- The registered manager's or senior's guidance on individual service users

## 7. What WorkTwin Can Answer

Ask WorkTwin may draw on this pathway to give **general routing guidance**, once governance approval is in place and the document is uploaded and indexed. WorkTwin may answer:

- **Who to contact** for a given type of concern (e.g. senior, registered manager, emergency services) at a general level.
- **When to escalate** — the kinds of situation that mean a worker should stop and contact a human rather than continue alone.
- **What route to follow** — the order of escalation (senior → registered manager → specialist/emergency route) described in this pathway.
- A reminder to **record real events** in Thumhara Centre's existing care-recording system, not in WorkTwin.
- A reminder of **what must never be entered** into WorkTwin.

Answers must be calm, short, practical and grounded in this approved pathway. WorkTwin must not invent contact details, names, or routes that are not in the approved document.

---

## 8. What WorkTwin Must Escalate

WorkTwin must stop attempting to give routing detail and direct the worker to a human immediately when the question involves any of the following. These are **escalation-guided** topics — WorkTwin points to the route and the human, it does not resolve the situation:

- Immediate danger or risk to life
- A person showing signs of illness, injury, deterioration or serious distress
- Welfare concern the worker cannot resolve
- A confidentiality or information-handling concern that may be a breach
- An incident or near-miss that has occurred
- The worker is unsure what to do and no clear route applies

For the following topics WorkTwin must **block direct guidance and route to the correct human/specialist** — it must not advise on the substance:

- **Medication** of any kind (timing, dose, administration, whether to give/withhold)
- **Safeguarding** assessment (whether something is abuse, neglect, or reportable)
- **Falls after an actual fall** / suspected injury
- **Epilepsy** / seizure response
- **Clinical procedures** of any kind
- **Complaints**
- **Whistleblowing** / raising concerns about practice
- **HR, absence or disciplinary** matters

## 9. Emergency Route

**Use when there is immediate danger or a risk to life.**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | If there is immediate danger, serious injury, collapse, breathing difficulty, severe pain, or any life-threatening sign — **call 999 immediately**. Do not wait for internal escalation. |
| 2 | Give first aid only within your training and the care plan. Do not attempt clinical procedures you are not trained for. |
| 3 | As soon as it is safe, inform your senior / shift lead and the registered manager. |
| 4 | Record the event factually in Thumhara Centre's care-recording system as soon as it is safe to do so. Follow the Accident and Incident Reporting procedure. |

**Emergency services:** **999**

## 10. Non-Emergency Escalation Route

**Use when there is a concern that needs a human decision but is not an emergency.**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Decide whether the person is safe right now. If not, use the Emergency Route (Section 9). |
| 2 | Contact your **senior / shift lead**. Describe clearly: what happened, what you observed, what you tried, and your current concern. |
| 3 | If the senior is unavailable, contact the **registered manager** via the agreed route. |
| 4 | Do not leave the call without informing the office if a risk remains. |
| 5 | Record the event and who you contacted, with times, in Thumhara Centre's care-recording system. |

**Senior / shift lead:** *[Contact details to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*
**Registered Manager:** *[Contact details to be confirmed by Thumhara Centre]*

| Situation | Staff action | Route |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Immediate danger / risk to life | Call 999 without delay; then notify office | **Emergency services — 999 → Senior → Registered Manager** |

## 17. HR / Absence / Disciplinary Exclusion

This pathway does **not** cover HR, staff absence, sickness, payroll, grievance, disciplinary, capability or conduct matters. WorkTwin must not advise on any of these.

Staff with an HR, absence or disciplinary matter must use Thumhara Centre's HR/people route and speak to their line manager. WorkTwin will route these questions to a human and will not attempt to answer them.

## 18. WorkTwin AI-Use Note

This section defines how Ask WorkTwin may be used with this pathway.

**WorkTwin may answer** (general routing only): who to contact, when to escalate, what route to follow, and reminders to record in the care system and not enter real data.

**WorkTwin must escalate / route to a human** for: immediate danger, illness/injury/deterioration, welfare concerns, confidentiality concerns, incidents, and any case where the worker is unsure.

**WorkTwin must block direct guidance and route to a specialist/human** for: medication, safeguarding, falls after an actual fall, epilepsy, clinical procedures, complaints, whistleblowing, and HR/absence/disciplinary matters.

**WorkTwin must always:** stay calm and short; name the correct human route clearly; never invent contact details, names or routes; never present general guidance as sufficient where a human judgement is required; cite this approved pathway as its source; and give no answer if no approved source is retrieved.

## 19. No-Real-Data Warning

The following must **never** be entered into WorkTwin at any time, including during controlled sandbox/demo testing:

- Real service-user names or identifiers
- Family or next-of-kin contact details
- Medication names, doses, schedules or instructions for a real person
- Care-plan details relating to a real service user
- Safeguarding details or allegation details
- Complaint details
- Incident details from a real situation
- HR, absence or disciplinary details
- Any information that would identify a real service user or staff member

All contact routes in this document are **synthetic placeholders**. Real records must be kept in Thumhara Centre's care management system, not in WorkTwin. WorkTwin is a staff guidance tool, not a record of care.
