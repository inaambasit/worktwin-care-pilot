# Privacy and Trust Principles

## Product promise

Private for the employee. Useful for the employer. Safe for both.

## Employee-private by default

The following should not be visible to the employer by default:
- private chats
- personal development notes
- confidence reflections
- draft messages
- sensitive questions
- private learning struggles

## Employer-visible

The employer can see:
- anonymised topic trends
- common policy confusion areas
- unanswered questions
- document improvement opportunities
- training gap themes
- escalation counts
- usage volumes at organisation/team level

## Forbidden product behaviours

Do not build:
- hidden performance scoring
- productivity surveillance
- sentiment scoring
- automated disciplinary advice
- automated dismissal/promotion recommendations
- individual risk labelling
- wellbeing diagnosis
- keystroke/mouse/screen monitoring

## Sensitive escalation areas

Escalate to a human for:
- safeguarding
- health and safety
- legal matters
- disciplinary issues
- grievances
- harassment
- payroll disputes
- medical/mental health concerns
- anything not supported by company documents

## Answering standard

The AI must:
- answer from approved documents only
- cite sources
- say when it cannot confirm
- recommend escalation where appropriate
- avoid making final employment, legal, medical or disciplinary decisions
