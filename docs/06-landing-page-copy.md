## Problem section

Your policies have the answers. Your staff still have to ask.

Most care providers already have handbooks, SOPs and training documents. The problem is that staff cannot always find the right answer, understand the process, or ask questions confidently without interrupting a manager.

This creates repeat questions, slower onboarding, avoidable mistakes and training gaps.

## Solution section

WorkTwin turns your approved documents into a private AI assistant for your staff.

Employees can ask questions, get source-cited answers, practise scenarios, take short knowledge checks and prepare for probation or supervision meetings.

Managers and leaders see anonymised insights showing where policies, training and onboarding need improving — not private staff conversations.

## Trust section

Not surveillance. Not performance scoring. Not a hidden monitoring tool.

WorkTwin is designed to support staff, not watch them. Private employee conversations are not shared with management by default. Employers receive anonymised trends so they can improve training, policies and documentation.
